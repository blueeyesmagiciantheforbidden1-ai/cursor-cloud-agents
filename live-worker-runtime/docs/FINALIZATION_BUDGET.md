# Per-lane finalization budgets and admission floors

Status: **T176b design only**. Light should present the alternatives below to the user before implementation. Variant (a) retains T176's conservative sum; (b) accounts for mutually exclusive outcomes; (c) adds aggregate operation budgets and an explicit failed-cleanup delivery path. Recommend (c), subject to its containment and deadline contracts. No runtime, test, hub policy, or transport change is made here. These are proposed budgets for attempted finalization, not measured latencies or a guarantee of delivery through unavailable infrastructure.

The supplied snapshots are worker d8f6d59 and reference hub 3f7bfd5 (`CONTEXT.md:2-3`), packaged in local base `7955b60`. ## Inputs and scope

Current metadata-authenticated hub calls have nominal envelope **H = (5+10)+(5+10) = 30**, including a cold identity fetch, request, and authentication retry (`entrypoint.py:132-134`; `cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/worker.py:251-254,283-291,314-319`). Cached calls can be shorter; the identity cache lasts 480 (`worker.py:233`). Completion permits three attempts with sleeps 1 and 2, hence **3H+1+2 = 93** (`live_loop.py:1305-1339`). Socket inactivity timeouts do not bound DNS, repeated reads, body close or serialization. The existing code has no strict whole-operation deadline.

A broker action has nominal client envelope **B = 15+15 = 30**: an uncached identity exchange followed by an action exchange (`cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/credential_broker_service.py:629-632,675-697`). Each exchange's socket timeout 10 overlaps its watchdog 15; DNS is excluded (`:107-153`). Successful finish requires assert-current, commit and release, hence **3B = 90**. Ordinary post-answer failure can invoke session quarantine then adapter quarantine, hence **2B = 60**. Sources for each lane's sequence are `credential_state_claude.py:57-80`, `credential_state_cursor.py:57-80`, `cca/agent-hub/deploy/codex-worker/credential_state.py:57-82`, `cca/agent-hub/deploy/copilot-worker/credential_state.py:59-82`, `cca/agent-hub/deploy/grok-worker/credential_state.py:59-82`; adapter fallbacks are `providers/claude.py:426-449`, `codex.py:417-449`, `copilot.py:614-649`, `cursor.py:330-354`, `grok.py:263-291`.

The quarantine accounting is scoped to ordinary post-answer cleanup. T172's pre-model/no-handle paths can make additional calls and need their own bounded coordinator (`T176-REVIEW.md:18`; `live_loop.py:896-932`; `providers/codex.py:417-449`). Do not spend this answer budget on an unbounded startup cleanup loop.

**A failed finish is not an alternative to all finish work.** Release can time out after assert-current and commit, so the failure branch includes the entire attempted finish prefix before quarantine. Current ordinary post-answer failed cleanup returns `credential_cleanup_failed` locally and then attempts worker telemetry; it does not send task failure completion (`live_loop.py:1641-1674,1699-1708,1745-1748`). The pre-prompt sign-out exception is narrower (`:1645-1668`). Thus (b) can save time only if it preserves that failure-reporting contract. Adding a full failure completion to a late failed finish would cost **90+60+93 = 243**, eliminating (b)'s saving. Variant (c) explicitly budgets this delivery with shorter whole-operation limits.

All variants retain the following T176 NEW inputs (`T176-REVIEW.md:7-11`) and the corresponding future implementation obligations:

S is not an enforced setup bound: actual admission must be checked again immediately before the prompt.

Renewal's new whole-operation cap preserves B above; credential I/O and coordination use the existing M scale. I/O covers validation/read/writeback preparation across session and adapter, after native stop. Coordination includes nonblocking outcome recording, cancellation and joining. These small local caps are policy choices to fault-inject, not proven filesystem/OS maxima. Never reset them on an exception or repeated close.
- Native allowance **N** is unchanged from T176: **Claude 19 = 5 tail + 10 exit + 2 reader joins + 1 aggregate writer joins + 1 pipes/validation** (`providers/claude.py:338-382`; the tail's NEW 5 uses the existing clean-exit scale at `codex-cloud-transport/metadata.py:404`). **Codex 15 = 7 finish-turn + 8 stop**, where finish-turn is 1 stdin/validation + 5 exit + 1 readers, and stop is 5 exit + 2 aggregate joins + 1 pipes (`codex-cloud-transport/transport.py:194-201`, `metadata.py:401-416`; `providers/codex.py:237-253`). **Copilot 22 = 5 getCurrent + 5 shutdown + 5 reap + 5 drain + 2 pipes/selector** (`providers/copilot.py:333-389,862-866`); the RPC caps are NEW choices matching the existing reap/drain scale. **Cursor 6 = 5 stop + 1 buffered validation/pipes** (`providers/cursor.py:488-495`; `cursor_native/metadata.py:170-179`). **Grok 11 = 10 stop + 1 validation/pipes/reader termination** (`providers/_grok_protocol.py:110-120,193-198`; `providers/grok.py:490-528`). Aggregate joins/local caps replace currently unbounded accumulated writer, stream and reader work; they are not claims that current cleanup is bounded.
- **Round10(x) = 10 * ceil(x/10)** is T176's NEW planning headroom (`T176-REVIEW.md:7`). For every table, **admission A = G+F+M = F+35**, **budget-derived hub floor U = A+S = F+65**, and proposed effective **hub minimum T = max(today, U)**. Retaining today's Claude minimum avoids lowering a separate generation policy merely because cleanup got faster (`runcrew/agent_hub/core.py:37-45`). The U column exposes the lower mathematical floor for the user's decision.

T175 already starts a background renewer before adapter-owned close and keeps it through delivery (`broker_renew.py:65-74`; `live_loop.py:1596-1606`). It does not yet supply the aggregate bounds above. Its hooks also follow the native answer tail in Claude/Copilot/Codex (`providers/claude.py:547`, `copilot.py:869`, `codex.py:665`); the proposed coordinator must begin at answer capture so native finalization is covered too. Remove serial finishing-beat/report work from the budgeted path; renew the task concurrently and defer optional telemetry. After the single strict boundary broker renewal, suppress synchronous broker renewals in post-answer native ticks; retained callbacks must fit the remaining N without resetting it. Current synchronous adapter/loop beats and reports (`live_loop.py:934-961,1153-1243`) cannot simply be counted as free while retained.

## Budget exhaustion and remote mutation contract

The following rules make (c)'s smaller budgets reviewable. Apply the same containment rules to (a)/(b)'s longer limits; their different failed-cleanup reporting is explicit above. Only acknowledged clean finish permits success delivery. Every stage deadline is monotonic and clipped to the shared outer finalization cutoff. Authentication, retries, DNS, serialization, response/error-body close and cancellation consume the owning budget; no nested operation receives a fresh budget on retry. A timed thread join does not establish cancellation. Use owned supervised helpers or an actually cancellable transport; include helper termination/join within the budget and never wait indefinitely in logging or shutdown.

1. **Boundary renewal 30 expires:** stop normal finish, retain the original credential fence, stop/contain the native process within N, and enter quarantine-then-failure reporting. Do not infer a renewed credential lease from a timed-out request; a remote renewal can still succeed, but it only extends the same ownership. No new model call or credential reuse follows uncertainty (`broker_renew.py:132-155`; `cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/credential_broker_service.py:678-697`).
2. **Native N or local credential I/O 5 expires:** cancel owned work; unconfirmed native stop or partial/uncertain credential reads prohibit commit/release and success completion. Quarantine using the current fence. If native stop remains unconfirmed, (c) uses its delivery slot only for a worker failure report and reconciliation notice; it must not send a task completion that could invite replacement while the process remains live. Record `credential_cleanup='failed'`, retain stopped/unknown status truthfully, and stop claiming. Isolation that cannot be established within the cap is a failed outcome, not proof the resource disappeared.
3. **Finish 45 expires mid assert/commit/release:** freeze the normal finish state machine, cancel the owned client helper, and reserve the separate quarantine 30 and delivery 45. Treat any dispatched mutation without a definitive receipt as uncertain. Do not retry commit under a new intent, issue a blind release, or turn a late acknowledgement into a successful answer. Record `credential_cleanup='failed'` and proceed quarantine-then-report, even if reconciliation later proves the release was clean. A pre-dispatch authentication timeout has no remote action, but the credential lease still needs safe cleanup (`credential_broker_service.py:675-697`).
4. **Quarantine 30 expires:** no further inline quarantine calls and no fresh adapter fallback budget. Retain the broker's actual state, record quarantine unconfirmed/reconciliation required, stop claiming/reusing credentials and use the reserved delivery slot. Do not claim that setting the adapter's local `quarantined` state proves a remote quarantine receipt. A non-idle broker state already prevents takeover even after lease expiry (`cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/cloud_credential_broker.py:549,557-566,815-821`). Operator reconciliation is explicit and requires terminal-execution evidence (`:823-850`); unavailable reporting can delay visibility, but must never change broker state to idle to manufacture progress.
5. **Delivery 45 expires:** stop retries/sleeps, cancel the client helper, preserve the exact immutable completion payload and mark `completion_delivery_uncertain` / `completion_delivery='unconfirmed'`. This applies to successful-answer delivery and task failure delivery; retain the cleanup failure fact separately. Budget expiry never means acknowledged failure or success. A remote `/complete` can have committed despite the missing response; the hub's same-token, identical-payload replay is idempotent, whereas a changed payload conflicts (`runcrew/agent_hub/core.py:2245-2251`; `live_loop.py:1301-1303`). Never switch to failure payload after uncertain success delivery or launch another model call locally. A definitive refusal keeps its existing refused/rejected classification (`live_loop.py:1315-1338`). Worker-report-only delivery has unconfirmed telemetry rather than invented task completion.
6. **Coordination 5 expires:** retain the terminal uncertain/failure record and credential fence; abandon optional telemetry/flush, do not extend F for helper joins or a second cleanup. Signal/cancel renewal concurrently; clip any join to the minimum of remaining coordination time, operation deadline and outer cutoff. Once exhausted, record the unjoined/containment failure without another wait, and never mark an unjoined native process stopped. Outcome reporting must expose any containment failure to the supervisor; an OS stall or disconnected supervisor cannot be represented as a guaranteed remotely received report.

For (c), after native stop is confirmed, failed cleanup may attempt a **NEW failure-only task completion**, with nonzero exit, `error_code='credential_cleanup_failed'`, truthful `model_call_attempted`, and explicit cleanup/reconciliation status in the failure text/outcome. This requires a narrowly scoped change to today's completion guard (`live_loop.py:1299-1300,1641-1674`), not setting `cleaned=True` or reusing the pre-prompt exception. Acknowledged failure records no successful answer and does not certify credential release. The hub currently classifies an attempted-call failure as `post_model` and can automatically retry in a read-only auto room (`runcrew/agent_hub/core.py:222-238,1035-1046,2384-2389`); the cleanup flag does not independently suppress recovery. A still-busy broker prevents credential reuse. If native stop cannot be confirmed, use the worker-report-only route above; preventing all replacement after eventual lease expiry would require a further hub/controller containment policy, not a claim supplied by these budgets.

**Fencing and first reason are essential, but do not undo remote effects.** In the supplied worker broker, `_owned` checks profile/account/execution/lease/fence (`cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/cloud_credential_broker.py:713-719`) and state writes use compare-and-swap (`:466-492`). `quarantine` is idempotent: an already quarantined state returns without replacing its reason (`:803-813`). Preserve the first reason actually persisted; the session's `writeback_uncertain` normally precedes the adapter's `provider_refresh_uncertain`, but concurrent requests are ordered by their winning writes, not caller intent. If quarantine wins, a delayed commit must recheck the same committing intent and cannot publish over it (`:776-782`); a delayed release cannot bypass the committed-state requirement (`:790-801`). A Secret Manager version creation may still finish and leave a reconciliation candidate/orphan; timeout does not roll it back. If release wins first, it already required a durable commit and verified native stop at the caller: same-lease quarantine of idle is a harmless no-op. If another acquisition then advances the fence, old quarantine/commit/release must fail ownership checks rather than touch the new lease (`:557-566,713-719,790-813`). Equivalent reference-hub rules are `runcrew/agent_hub/cloud_credential_broker.py:433-464,513-548`.

The worker broker snapshot has separate pre/post-mutation server budgeting (`cca/agent-hub/agent_hub/cloud_credential_broker.py:30-42,94-116,761-788`), unlike the older reference broker. Its server policy is not a client whole-finish bound and must not be changed incidentally. In **all three variants**, remote effects can outlive the client's cap; budget expiry bounds supervisor waiting, not distributed mutation lifetime. None permits silent release, automatic credential reuse after uncertainty, or a claim that a timed-out quarantine definitely persisted.

## Admission and phase deadlines

Map the hub's absolute task deadline conservatively to monotonic D, subtracting elapsed request time as today (`live_loop.py:1252-1264`). The selected lane contract is:

```text
admit only if D - now >= G + F + M
response_cutoff = D - F - M
finalization_cutoff = D - M
```

Check early, then immediately before the native prompt after model-call heartbeat, broker renewal/assertion, metadata and prompt-file preparation. Reject insufficient time with `task_deadline_insufficient` before setting `model_call_attempted` (`live_loop.py:1568-1579`); do not move phase backward. Apply the same contract to warm admission (`:1345-1357`) and fake adapters. F already includes completion: replace the legacy R=25 (allowed 15..30, `:321,330`) and Codex reserve45 (`providers/codex.py:40,634`) atomically, not cumulatively. Preserve the lane generation maximum, including Codex's existing 600 cap (`providers/codex.py:40,634`).

A response received by response_cutoff gets the reserved native finalization allowance after the boundary-renewal slot. Copilot's getCurrent/shutdown/reap/drain must use this later native deadline instead of the response deadline; drain must honor outer cancellation and renewal (`providers/copilot.py:351-389,849,862-866`). Claude EOF validation and Codex finish_turn need the same separation. The old deadline cannot remain in their internal tick paths. Share each N across success/failure cleanup without restarting waits. With (c), the longest boundary-renew/native/I/O/finish/quarantine path is **30+22+5+45+30 = 132**, below the existing credential lease240; even (a)/(b)'s corresponding **30+22+5+90+60 = 207** fits (`broker_renew.py:22`; N and operation inputs above). This assumes an acknowledged strict boundary renewal and conservative start anchor, not tolerance of an unconfirmed renewal.

All variants preserve stored room/task deadlines. Raising minima governs newly created rooms and actor claim/recovery eligibility; it does not grant extra time to an existing lease. Worker admission remains authoritative for short existing rooms and rollback. Explicit Cursor300 at `runcrew/agent_hub/study_bridge.py:158` needs changing under (a), but fits (b)/(c); fixture/caller changes must follow the chosen policy, not blanket increases. Raising T reduces the time window in which another attempt remains eligible before the room wall; it does not increase retry counts. Renewable expiry, absolute task expiry, and room wall remain distinct.

## Renewer timing (T175)

Current **FINISHING_RENEW_INTERVAL_SECONDS = 15** and **HUB_POST_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 10** are at `live_loop.py:28-31`; the renewer waits the interval after the previous request returns (`:1052-1068`). Only an active acknowledgement advances its request-start anchor (`:1062-1070`). Hub lifetime is **45**, clamped to D, and settlement happens before ownership validation/renewal (`runcrew/agent_hub/core.py:50,2149-2153`). Therefore budget the delay of the previous acknowledgement as well as the next request.

**(i) Interval 10 alone does not hold.** Even treating the nominal H30 as a hard limit, conservative request-start accounting gives **30+10+30 = 70 > 45** (current interval gives **30+15+30 = 75**). A tighter counterexample using previous response delay10 plus interval10 plus cached authentication-retry25 reaches **45** with no safety margin. The previous T176 proposal **15+10+15 = 40** works only with an additional NEW whole-call cap15; changing cadence alone does not impose that cap. Present DNS/read/close behavior remains unbounded regardless of this arithmetic.

**Recommend (ii): a dedicated renewal client with one whole-operation cap 5, keeping interval15.** Derive 5 from the current metadata timeout scale (`worker.py:251`); it is a NEW aggressive deadline, not a latency guarantee. The worst healthy acknowledgement interval is **5+15+5 = 25 < 45**, leaving **20** for scheduling slack. Merely setting the HTTP opener timeout to5 is insufficient: metadata and an auth retry still give **(5+5)*2 = 20**, hence **20+15+20 = 55 > 45**. The cap must include metadata, all auth attempts, DNS, reads, response close and cancellation, with each socket timeout clipped to remaining time. A 401/403 retry gets only unused time; metadata consuming the entire5 leaves no POST budget. A timeout never counts as renewal.

T175 already prefers a separate client (`live_loop.py:976-993`); give that client its own deadline-aware transport rather than changing the completion client's timeout. Cache authentication when valid, but retain the bound for cold/expired tokens. At entry and on every attempt, clip cap to **min(5, last_ack_request_start+45-M-now, finalization_cutoff-now)**; start immediately when the inherited acknowledgement is too old to wait15. No nonpositive-budget POST is allowed. One failed round can consume **5+15+5+15+5 = 45**, so continuing blindly at the full cadence after failure is unsafe: recompute against the last confirmed anchor, retry only within it, otherwise report renewal uncertainty and keep containment. This is a conditional healthy-renewal bound, never a guarantee through hub/auth outages.

A late remote heartbeat might have renewed the same lease, but it does not advance the local acknowledged anchor. An in-flight heartbeat after definitive completion may receive409 and must not overwrite accepted completion (`live_loop.py:1040-1047,1073-1080`). Stop/join the coordinator within the remaining common coordination allowance, clipped to its operation deadline and the outer cutoff; today's join **10+1 = 11** (`:343,1016-1025`) is not the proposed bound and a join timeout alone does not stop a request. Preserve SIGTERM handling and absolute D; do not revive an expired/replaced lease.

## Recommendation

Recommend **(c)** with finish45, quarantine30 and aggregate delivery45, plus the independent renewal whole-call cap5. Its failure branch includes spent finish time and a real failure-reporting allowance; it avoids raising the full-fleet default above today's300 while retaining Claude's existing minimum. The price is explicit cutoff-driven failures and reconciliation when infrastructure is slow, plus a carefully tested new failure-only delivery path. (b) is the smaller implementation change if retaining delayed cleanup-failure visibility is acceptable; (a) reserves the most time but still cannot make unbounded operations safe. Light should present these policy and reporting tradeoffs to the user; no variant is authorized for runtime rollout by this document.
